THE MEMORY MUSEUM

Every fish represents a cherished memory from the past.

第一：童年 CHILDHOOD

Shared celebrations.

Every Midwestern birthday's favorite: Pineapple-Upside-Down Cake. My Dad grew up with it, as his parents did. Within the sweet bites is filled with laughter, love, and care.

第二：友谊 FRIENDSHIP

Our beginnings, together.

We all started somewhere, and grew up to be someone. Do you remember your first friend? What about their character? How have they changed over time? Have you?

第三：好奇心 CURIOSITY

Search for the knowledge you desire.

The entrance to a world filled with infinite knowledge. Childhood is filled with innocent curiosity. It's a misconception that adults can't maintain that curiosity as well.

第四：冒险 ADVENTURE

Discovering the unknown world.

Discovering the elements of a world unknown to small eyes. Adventure can be filled with mystery, surprise, sadness, happiness, and a multitude of other emotions. Take it as it goes.

第五：希望 HOPE

Tomorrow's dream.

The time yet to pass still holds potential. Even when things seem hopeless, the future is unpredictable. Your decisions are held in your own hands. Don't give up on yourself.
